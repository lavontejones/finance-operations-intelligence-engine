# Case study: Northstar Build & Fabrication LLC

Northstar is a fictional single-company business with approximately $25M annual revenue and 100 employees. Its work includes construction delivery and light fabrication. The case models common finance operations: project billing, supplier purchases, payroll, fleet cost, capital equipment, and debt service.

## Problem framing

Finance teams need a way to receive source events, detect suspicious or duplicate items, reconcile bank activity, route uncertainty to people, and create a repeatable cash and close view. A spreadsheet-only process can work for small volumes, but it becomes difficult to trace evidence and decisions as activity increases.

## Reference approach

The engine first validates and retains a source reference. It then applies explainable rules. Clear results become controlled proposals. Unclear or risky results enter a review queue. Reporting reads from the controlled results and still labels itself as illustrative.

## Evidence in this repository

- Duplicate fuel-card payment: review queue example.
- Large capital-equipment purchase: anomaly investigation example.
- Unmatched bank activity: reconciliation example.
- AR bucket, 13-week cash view, budget variance, and CFO-package output: planning examples.

No client result, client integration, live deployment, or performance claim is made.
